<h1>MountMaster</h1>

MountMaster is a mount collectors best friend.
It's a mount journal, with loads of sorting options, like expansions, categories and difficulties.

<hr/>

<h2>Features</h2>

Expansions<br/>
Select General, All or a Specific WoW expansion to view mounts from it. Can be found on the left side.

Difficulty<br/>
Useful sorting to find help Easy to pick up or Hard grinding mounts to accuire next.

Categories<br/>
Sorting based on the mount sources. Find Vicious, AOTC, Rares and many more catory mounts categories.

3D models<br/>
Show casing the mounts in all their glory with 3D models, this feature can be disabled in settings.

<hr/>
<h2>Community feedback wanted</h2>

Currently the mounts are mostly filled with AI generated mount tips and difficulty ratings.
This is a starting point, temporary data with the goal to have custom tips based on the community for every single mount in the game. Mount information have an Author field where you can get your nickname showcased for all to see
